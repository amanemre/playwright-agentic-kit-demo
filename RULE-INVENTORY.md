# Rule inventory

Phase A deliverable of `GENERALIZATION-PLAN-AGREED.md` (revision 2). It classifies every rule of the constitution (`CLAUDE.md`, mirrored in `.cursor/rules/rules.mdc` and `.github/copilot-instructions.md`) and every rule in the skills' Critical blocks. Since Phase C every constitution row and every preset-default bullet in the skills carries its ID from this inventory.

Inventoried on 2026-10-05 (Phase A; tags and wording aligned in Phase C): 39 constitution rules (17 MUST, 5 SHOULD, 17 WON'T) and 121 Critical bullets in 14 skills (`playwright-cli`, `pr-reviewer` and `skill-creator` have none). Every one of them is assigned below.

| Column          | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

## Project facts

Project facts are not rules. Phases B–C moved them out of the constitution and the skills into `PROJECT-PROFILE.md`: URLs and env var names, the area folder, the API's conventions (where the outcome is reported, request encoding, authentication), starting-state helpers, environment problems, commands, known-defect baselines and worked examples. General files describe how to find such facts, never their values.

## Examples

General skills use one fictional **Example Shop** (`POST /api/users/login`, `POST /api/users`, `GET /api/products`, `generateUser()`, a `createdUser` helper fixture), clearly marked illustrative. The project's real examples are listed in the profile's "Worked examples" section.

## Findings for later phases

1. **Hook-only checks could be bypassed** — resolved in Phase E: the same checks run from `scripts/rule-check.mjs` in the Claude hook, the pre-commit hook and CI.
2. **Hardcoded preset paths in checks** — resolved in Phase E: every DEF check reads its paths and settings from the profile and switches off when the profile records an override.
3. **Mostly review-only.** 32 of the 47 rules (12 INV, 13 FW, 22 DEF) are checked by review alone, including 11 of the 12 invariants; the other 15 have at least a partial lint, hook or script check. Only a few can become mechanical checks (plan validator in Phase D; profile-aware lint rules in Phase E); the rest stay evidence review, which the plan accepts explicitly (§2).
4. **One rule mixed two categories.** "Sources of Truth" combined an invariant (no hardcoded secrets — INV-04) with preset conventions (enums layout — DEF-10); since Phase C the row names both parts with their IDs.
